# 模型假设台账

状态：已冻结。本表只保留当前正式主模型实际使用的普通设定；题面明确条件与已确认结算口径不在此重复列为假设。

| ID | 假设陈述 | 来源 | 验证或挑战路径 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A07-JAN | January is a causal warm-up with storage idle, so 2025-02-01 00:00 SOC equals 6000 kWh. | A07；用户确认 | Compare opening SOC and downstream cash cost under a replayed January operation. |
| M01 | A supplied right-endpoint power value represents the mean power of its ten-minute natural-day interval, giving energy P/6. | M01 | Compare the Q1 rectangle rule with trapezoidal integration without changing the disclosed A01 mapping. |
| M02 | Hourly PV forecasts are linearly interpolated to nonnegative ten-minute values using only information already available at issue time. | M02 | Compare linear interpolation and step hold by forecast error and realized cost. |
| M03 | Storage feedback acts at ten-minute resolution and only the current-slot action is executed. | M03 | Audit every action against its information cutoff timestamp. |
| M04 | Charging and discharging are mutually exclusive within a ten-minute interval. | M04 | Check simultaneous positive flows and use an exact mutually-exclusive formulation if material flow appears. |
| M05 | PV may be curtailed, no sale revenue is included, and unused purchased energy remains paid. | M05 | Independently reconcile every ten-minute supply balance and surplus category. |
| M06 | The cash-cost base model includes no self-discharge, line loss or battery degradation charge beyond stated efficiencies. | M06 | Report efficiency and degradation scenarios separately from the requested cash cost. |
| M07 | The base model adds no unprovided external-grid capacity limit. | M07 | Treat grid caps only as an additional stress scenario. |
| M08 | Forecast estimation and evaluation proceed in chronological rolling order without future-data leakage. | M08 | Audit training cutoffs and compare rolling MAE/RMSE/WAPE with realized operating cost. |
| M09 | Q2-Q4 carry SOC across days and use an explicit moving-horizon terminal treatment rather than a daily SOC reset. | M09 | Report final SOC and compare common terminal treatments or inventory-value corrections. |
| M10 | Internal ledgers retain full precision; reported energy uses four decimals, cost uses two decimals, and positive-event tolerance is 1e-7 kWh. | M10 | Recompute totals from unrounded ten-minute ledgers. |
| Q2-forecast | Q2 load uses chronological linearly weighted same-weekday history and PV uses the prior five completed days at the same slot. | 已冻结Q2主模型 | The recorded lightweight robustness matrix and independent recomputation support this fixed setting. |
| Q2-residual-quantile | Q2 uses the 80% quantile of prior causal net-load residuals as a correction amount. | 已冻结Q2主模型及费用结构 | Executed ablation and risk-level comparison support the selected correction without claiming a strict critical fractile. |
| Q2-horizon | At each midnight Q2 plans 48 hours, executes day one, and carries realized SOC into the next day. | 已冻结Q2主模型 | Compare the fixed terminal treatment with the stated terminal sensitivity record. |
| Q3-update | At 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 Q3 updates only unexecuted slots using the latest PV forecast, actual SOC and alpha=0.5 causal load feedback. | 已冻结Q3主模型 | Information-cutoff audit, independent recomputation and alpha calibration record validate the setting. |
| Q3-settlement | Q3 settles final effective normal purchase once against the 00:00 plan under the frozen A08 interpretation. | 已冻结Q3主模型 | Independent ten-minute cost recomputation and the recorded fixed-trajectory sensitivity provide the challenge path. |
| Q4-price | At 00:00 Q4 forecasts the price profile from prior same-weekday history; planning uses forecast prices and settlement uses actual delivery-interval prices. | 已冻结Q4主模型 | Chronological price-prediction validation and fixed-strategy repricing versus reoptimization check the setting. |
| Q4-intraday-price | The intraday-adjustment dynamic-price branch keeps the 00:00 price profile unchanged at 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 while updating PV, SOC and alpha=0.5 load feedback. | 已冻结Q4主模型 | The recorded 2×2 comparison checks the absence of intraday price correction and information cutoffs. |
